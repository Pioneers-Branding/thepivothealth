Introduction

Last updated: November 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at Virtus Growth Labs Pvt. Ltd., operating through our health and wellness website www.thepivothealth.com ("Virtus", "Pivot", "Pivot Health", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and your right to privacy.

This Privacy Policy applies to all personal data collected through our website (www.thepivothealth.com), mobile application, chat applications (e.g., WhatsApp), advertisements and promotions on any social media channels or mediums, enquiry and assessment forms or questionnaires, social media accounts, email survey forms, email, and/or any related services, sales, marketing, or events, the website of our parent company (www.velocityx.in), and the purchase of our Program(s) and/or plans (together, the "Services").

This Policy is our notice to you under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (the "DPDP Rules"). It is an electronic record and does not require a physical or digital signature.

We process your personal data only for the purposes described below and, where the law requires it, only after you give your consent through a clear affirmative action (for example, ticking a consent checkbox on a form or choosing your cookie preferences). Simply visiting or browsing this website does not mean you have consented to optional processing such as marketing or non-essential cookies.

2. What Personal Data Do We Collect?

Through this website:

  • Enquiry / contact forms: name, email address, mobile number (where asked), programme of interest, your message, and your consent choices.
  • Heart Health Assessment: name, mobile number, city, email address, age, gender, height, weight, blood pressure, blood test values (e.g., cholesterol, triglycerides, glucose, HbA1c, vitamin levels), lifestyle factors (e.g., smoking, alcohol, diet, activity, sleep, stress), family history, menopause status, current medications, the calculated risk score, and your consent choices.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: strictly necessary data, and—only if you opt in—analytics and marketing data (see Section 14).

When you enrol in or use our Programmes and other Services:

  • Personal identification data (e.g., name, email address, phone number, birth date, gender, picture, photographs, mailing address)
  • Health-related data (e.g., any reports or scans, doctor's prescriptions, hospital records, blood reports data, weight, height, food preferences, nutrition data, workouts, steps, sleep data, SPO2, HRV, body temperature, medical conditions, health goals)
  • Payment information (only name and billing address, not card details)
  • Automatically collected data (e.g., IP address, device and browser information, usage patterns, approximate location, metadata)
  • Data from third-party devices, mobile and messaging apps (e.g., geo-location, device access, push notifications), only where you enable these
  • Information from third-party integrations (e.g., HealthKit, Google Fit, Apple Health, email, social media login data)
  • Data from integrated health apps and devices: if you connect your Apple Watch or Apple Health, Morpheus Training, or similar applications with our Services, we may access certain health metrics (e.g., heart rate, HRV, step count, activity data, sleep). This data is accessed only with your explicit consent and is used solely to provide wellness recommendations and progress tracking.
  • Your contributions: content, communications and other information you provide when you sign up for an account, create or share content, or message us.

We collect only the personal data that is necessary for the purposes stated in this Policy.

5. Consent and Withdrawal

Where we rely on consent, your consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, and given through a clear affirmative action. On our website this is done by ticking the consent checkbox shown with each form (each with a short privacy notice) and by choosing your cookie preferences in the cookie banner. Optional purposes, such as marketing, have a separate checkbox that is not ticked by default.

Withdrawing consent: you can withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it — by emailing ankur@thepivothealth.com, by using the unsubscribe option in our messages, or (for cookies) through the link in the website footer.

Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it. After withdrawal we will stop processing your personal data for that purpose (and ask our processors to do the same) within a reasonable time, unless retention is required by law. If you withdraw consent needed to deliver a Service, we may no longer be able to provide that Service to you.

12. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, you (the "Data Principal") have the right to:

  • Access information — a summary of the personal data we process about you, the processing activities, and the identities of others with whom it has been shared
  • Correction, completion and updating of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure of personal data that is no longer needed, unless retention is required by law
  • Withdraw consent at any time (see Section 5)
  • Grievance redressal through our Grievance Officer, and thereafter to complain to the Data Protection Board of India

To exercise any of these rights, email ankur@thepivothealth.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. You are also expected to provide accurate information and not to file false or frivolous complaints, as set out in Section 15 of the DPDP Act.

14. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. When you first visit our website, a cookie banner asks for your choice. Only strictly necessary cookies are used unless you opt in. Accepting and rejecting are equally easy.

Category Purpose Default
Strictly necessary Security, page delivery and form submission, and remembering your cookie choice (stored in your browser for up to 6 months). Always on
Analytics Understanding how visitors use the site so we can improve it (e.g., Google Analytics). Off until you opt in
Marketing Measuring our advertising and showing relevant ads on other platforms (e.g., Meta Pixel, Google Ads). Off until you opt in

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using (also in the website footer). You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work properly if strictly necessary cookies are blocked.
